### Grupos de issues y arreglos
1. **44× `unnecessary_non_null_assertion`**: `AppLocalizations.of(context)!` → `AppLocalizations.of(context)` (16 archivos, script python). Redundante porque `l10n.yaml` usa `nullable-getter: false`.
2. **8× `unused_import`** + **1× `unnecessary_import`**: removidos `dart:typed_data` (upload_batch_notifier, data_room_explorer), `file_entity.dart` (i_folder_repository), `constants.dart` (data_room_lobby), `app_theme.dart` (dynamic_watermark_text), `app_localizations.dart` (file_list_tile), `i_data_room_repository.dart` y `local_database_provider.dart` (main.dart), `batch_upload_to_folder_usecase.dart` (explorer, ya provisto por upload_batch_notifier).
